About

I run client work remotely from anywhere and build AI and automation systems for small businesses that hold up under audit. Fourteen years across France, Italy, Spain, Switzerland, and Sweden taught me to run projects with people I rarely share a room with.

I hold a PhD in Computational Chemistry and have spent seven years across AI strategy, ERP, SaaS, and data transformation, including Odoo ERP rollouts and a data science role with temporary CTO responsibilities. Today I run automation inside a regulated organisation, where audits are part of daily work.

My thesis is simple: alignment first, systems second. AI only where it creates real value, with governance built in from day one. Practical frameworks, no hype.

I sail, not especially well, and I run projects the way I navigate: logbook, bearings, no heroics.
