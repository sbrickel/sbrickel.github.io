3. Third-Party Services

This website integrates the following third-party services that may process personal data:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting) - GitHub, Inc., USA. Subject to GitHub's Privacy Policy. Data may be transferred to the USA under standard contractual clauses.
  • Cal.com (booking system) - Cal.com, Inc., USA. When you book an intro conversation, Cal.com processes your name, email address, and any answers to booking questions. Cal.com connects to Google Calendar to check availability. See Cal.com's Privacy Policy.
  • Tally.so (contact form) - Tally SRL, Belgium. The in-page form pop-up loads only after you accept non-essential cookies; without consent, the contact button links to Tally's own website instead. Data submitted via the form is processed by Tally. See Tally's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube / Google (embedded video, youtube-nocookie.com) - Google Ireland Ltd, Ireland. The video player loads only when you actively click to load it; until then, no request is made to YouTube. The nocookie domain further limits tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (web font delivery) - Google Ireland Ltd. Loading the Montserrat typeface from Google's CDN transmits your IP address to Google for content delivery. No cookies are set. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and the LOPDGDD, you have the right to:

  • Access - request a copy of the personal data I hold about you
  • Rectification - correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") - request deletion of your data where no legitimate retention basis applies
  • Restriction - request that I limit how your data is processed
  • Portability - receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection - object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent - at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at remoteconsultant.sebastian@gmail.com. I will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Authority: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid.
